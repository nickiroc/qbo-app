The QuickBooks Reporting Connector (the "Connector") is a private, internal integration built and operated by the business that owns it (the "Company", "we", "us"). It is not offered to the public and is not listed on the Intuit App Store.

2. Who may use it

Only the Company and its authorized staff, and only with the Company's own QuickBooks Online company. No license is granted to anyone else.

3. What it does

The Connector reads accounting data from QuickBooks Online: accounts and balances, vendors, bills, bill payments, vendor credits, checks, expenses, credit-card charges and ledger reports. It uses that data for internal financial reporting. It is read-only: it never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks.

4. Disconnecting

A QuickBooks administrator can disconnect the Connector at any time from the Apps section of QuickBooks Online. After that it can no longer read data.
