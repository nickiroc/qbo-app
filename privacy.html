Privacy policy

QuickBooks Reporting Connector · Last updated November 22, 2026

What data the Connector reads

From the Company's own QuickBooks Online company only: accounts and balances, vendors, bills, bill payments, vendor credits, checks, expenses, credit-card charges and ledger reports. These records can include vendor names and contact details as entered in QuickBooks.

How it is used

Only for the Company's internal financial reporting.

Where it is stored

In the Company's private, access-controlled cloud environment, encrypted in transit and at rest. Connection credentials are kept in an encrypted secrets store. Access is limited to authorized staff.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is processed only by the service providers needed to run the reports: Intuit (QuickBooks Online) and the Company's cloud hosting provider.

Retention and removal

Data is kept while it is needed for the Company's records and reporting. Once the Connector is disconnected, no new data is read, and stored copies can be deleted on request by the Company's administrator.

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics or tracking, and collect nothing from visitors.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
